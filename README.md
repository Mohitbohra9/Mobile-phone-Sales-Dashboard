# 📱 Mobile Phone Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Power BI Business Intelligence Project

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze mobile phone sales performance across brands, months, customer age groups, cities, payment methods and day-of-week trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to provide management with a consolidated view of mobile phone sales performance and identify key business drivers.

The dashboard helps answer:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- Which brands contribute the most sales?
- Which months perform best?
- Which customer age groups generate the highest sales?
- Which cities are the strongest markets?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- Which days of the week generate higher sales?
- Where should management focus sales and marketing efforts?

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

## 1. Monthly Sales Performance

July recorded the highest displayed monthly sales at **70M**, while September recorded the lowest at **57M**.

### Business Insight

The variation suggests that sales performance changes significantly across months.

Management should investigate:

- Seasonal demand
- Marketing campaigns
- Product launches
- Discounts
- Inventory availability

---

## 2. Brand Performance

Apple leads the displayed brand-share analysis with **30.36%**.

| Brand | Sales Share |
|---|---:|
| Apple | 30.36% |
| Samsung | 20.53% |
| Vivo | 19.64% |
| Xiaomi | 18.61% |
| OnePlus | 10.87% |

### Business Insight

Apple is the strongest displayed brand, making it an important contributor to overall sales.

However, the other brands collectively represent a significant portion of the business and provide opportunities for growth.

---

## 3. Customer Age Analysis

The **46+ customer segment** generates the highest displayed sales at **250M**.

| Age Group | Sales |
|---|---:|
| 46+ | 250M |
| 26–35 | 189M |
| 36–45 | 182M |
| 18–25 | 148M |

### Business Insight

The 46+ segment is an important customer group.

The lower sales contribution from the 18–25 segment could also represent an opportunity for targeted customer acquisition campaigns.

---

## 4. Geographic Analysis

Delhi leads the displayed Top 5 cities with **204M**, followed by Mumbai with **127M**.

### Business Insight

Sales are concentrated in major cities.

Management can use this information for:

- Inventory planning
- Regional marketing
- Sales-team allocation
- City-specific promotions

---

## 5. Day-of-Week Analysis

Saturday records the highest displayed daily sales at **115M**, while Wednesday records the lowest at **105M**.

### Business Insight

The business can use day-level trends to optimize:

- Promotional campaigns
- Sales staffing
- Inventory availability
- Marketing timing

---

## 6. Payment Method Analysis

| Payment Method | Share |
|---|---:|
| UPI | 26.36% |
| Debit Card | 24.72% |
| Credit Card | 24.69% |
| Cash | 24.22% |

### Business Insight

The payment mix is relatively balanced, with UPI having the highest displayed share.

Maintaining multiple payment options can help support customer convenience.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Investigate Monthly Fluctuations

Understand why July performs strongly and September performs relatively weakly.

### 2. Protect High-Performing Brands

Continue supporting Apple while identifying growth opportunities for Samsung, Vivo, Xiaomi and OnePlus.

### 3. Target Customer Segments

Use age-group insights to develop targeted marketing campaigns.

### 4. Focus on High-Value Cities

Prioritize Delhi and Mumbai for inventory and marketing planning.

### 5. Optimize Promotional Timing

Use day-of-week performance to plan promotions and staffing.

### 6. Monitor Sales Performance

Use Power BI to continuously monitor sales KPIs and identify performance gaps.
